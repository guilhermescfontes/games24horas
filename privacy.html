Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains how Games24horas (the "App") handles data. The App is a desktop tool that runs on the user's own computer. We do not run servers that collect user data.

1. Data the App handles

  • TikTok account connection: when you sign in with TikTok Login Kit, the App receives an access token, a refresh token and your TikTok open ID, plus basic profile information (such as display name and avatar) if you grant it.
  • Videos: the clips you choose to upload.

2. How the data is used

The tokens are used for one purpose: uploading the videos you approve to your own TikTok inbox as drafts, and checking the status of those uploads. The App does not read your followers, messages, or other people's content.

3. Where the data is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only in local files on your computer. They are not sent to us or to any third party. Videos are sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, when you approve an upload.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies in the App.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your computer until you delete it. To remove everything, delete the App's data folder. To revoke access, open TikTok settings and remove the App under apps and services permissions; the stored tokens then stop working.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data at any time because it lives on your own device. If you need help or want to make a request, including under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD), contact us.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone who is not allowed to hold a TikTok account.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
