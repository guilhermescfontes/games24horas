Games24horas

Gaming live stream highlights, edited and ready to post

A desktop tool for the Games24horas clips channel.

Games24horas is a desktop application that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It finds the best moments of gaming live streams, edits them into vertical short videos with captions, and lets the owner review each clip in a local approval panel.

How it works with TikTok

  • The channel owner signs in with their own TikTok account using TikTok Login Kit.
  • After the owner approves a clip in the local panel, the app uploads the video to that account's TikTok inbox as a draft, using the Content Posting API.
  • The owner opens the TikTok app, reviews the draft, writes the caption and publishes it manually.

The app never publishes anything on its own, and it does not read TikTok profiles, followers, messages or videos from other people.

Content

Clips are made only from live streams whose creators have given permission, and every clip credits the original streamer.
